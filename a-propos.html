profile

I am an architecture student at UCLouvain LOCI, on the Brussels Saint-Gilles campus. I completed my bachelor’s degree in architecture in 2026 and am currently spending a gap year exploring work in an architecture practice.

The way I approach a project rarely starts from form. I look first at uses: who already lives in or passes through a place, how they settle into it, but also how users make it their own and develop their own ways of inhabiting the space. I also try to understand what the site still holds of the practices that came before it. A space carries the memory of those who occupied it, of the uses that grew there and of the appropriations that gradually shaped its character.

This close reading of the context, the uses and the ways people inhabit a place is where I look for the starting point of a project. In this way I try to develop an architecture attentive to its context, its users and the different timescales of a place, while staying open to the constraints and opportunities particular to each project.

skills

I am developing an approach to design based on the analysis of context, of uses and of the different scales of the territory.

I work on architectural design and representation through 3D modelling, drawing, axonometrics and collage. I also use parametric design as a tool for exploration, to test variants and to support the development of a project.

These approaches are complemented by structural and environmental analysis, so that the constraints and the qualities of the site feed into the design process.
